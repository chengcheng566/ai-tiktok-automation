Venus: Where a Day is Longer Than a Year!

**SCRIPT_ENGLISH**  
Did you know that a single day on Venus—meaning the time it takes Venus to complete one spin on its axis—lasts about 243 Earth days? That’s longer than its year, which is just 225 Earth days. So if you were standing on Venus, you’d have to wait longer for a sunrise than for the planet to complete a full orbit around the Sun! And that’s not even counting its slow, retrograde rotation—Venus spins backward, so the Sun rises in the west. Pretty mind‑blowing, right?
